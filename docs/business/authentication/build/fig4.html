Every change to Sign-in Settings needs a second person
1
Maker submits
Switches single sign-on On or Off, or edits the connection details, with an optional reason. The switch and the connection details are always separate requests. Nothing changes on the Maker’s click.
2
Pending Checker approval
The current setting stays in effect. The screen is locked and shows the pending badge. One open request at a time; the Maker cannot approve their own.
The Checker decides
ApprovedApplies at once. The login page shows or hides “Login with SSO” with no refresh. Approver and time recorded.
RejectedNothing changes.
ExpiredNot reviewed within 24 hours. Nothing changes.
On holdParked by the Checker. The screen stays locked.
