A directory account, from first role to removal
1
Portal · Super Admin
Role published
Creating or editing a role in Role Management publishes it to the bank’s directory. Status: Published, or Failed with Retry.
2
Bank directory
Person assigned
The bank’s directory administrator assigns the person to a published portal role.
3
Portal · automatic
Account created
At the next sync — about every 40 minutes, or on demand. Source: Directory. Status: Active.
4
Portal · Super Admin
Entitlements assigned
Channel, approval level, product type, access to personal data. Until then the person can sign in but sees no data.
5
Bank directory
Changes and leavers
Name, department, phone and role follow the directory. Disabled there → Inactive here; the record is kept.
6
Bank directory
Permanent removal
30 days after deletion in the directory, the portal record is purged — with its audit entries.
