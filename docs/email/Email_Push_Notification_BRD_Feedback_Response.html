COMMERCIAL POSITION
The approved CR covers the current scope only, as described in the BRD: the ten agreed Push IDs for Credit Card, Personal Loan and CASA, in English and Arabic, on iOS and Android, the deep link into the Appro journey, the automatic retry when the Push API call fails, and regression testing.
•The original scope of work covers Email and SMS only. Updates to Email and SMS for new features are already part of the product.
•Push notification is new and bank-specific, so it is delivered as a product customisation. To fit the timeline, the scope is kept to an MVP that gets the feature live — for example, push content is not managed in Super Portal in this phase.
•The design is scalable, so the feature can be upgraded on top of this customisation after release.
Of note, moving forwards, the Bank does not need to worry about the CR concept anymore. Starting from October, Reem Bank moves to our dedicated development team model. The Bank approves one man-day package a year, and a dedicated squad works full-time for Reem Bank, backed by our specialists whenever needed. The Bank simply raises and signs off the requirement as today, and we start building right away, with no quotation and no waiting for budget approval, plus a monthly report on how many man-days have been used. For Push Notification, this means we go live on the agreed MVP scope, and the dedicated team can then pick up further enhancements based on real customer feedback, with no CR in the way. This is how we keep the solution future-proof, as you rightly highlighted.
