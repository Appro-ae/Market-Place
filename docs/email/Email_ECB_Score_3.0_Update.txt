Appro
Once Reem Bank and Expleo have verified the build in UAT, Appro can deploy to production. We will release after the bureau go-live, not before — deploying ahead of it would put Score 3.0 codes in front of credit users while the bureau is still returning Score 2.1 values.
No production impact today. With the Rule Engine configured as it is now and the AECB response structured as it is now, this change does not alter any live decision. It adds range codes, one new variable and two new attributes — it retires nothing and remaps nothing.
The impact is on how credit policy is set up. Score 3.0 is a new calibration with two scorecards on different scales — a score of 600 is High Risk on Mature and Low Risk on New to Credit. Cut-offs, tier mapping and any eligibility rule that quotes a score value need to be rewritten against the new bands, and New-to-Credit has no Very Low Risk band at all. Sections 9 to 11 of the BRD set out what we need confirmed.
Updated data points and the upgraded report format will follow in the October release.
