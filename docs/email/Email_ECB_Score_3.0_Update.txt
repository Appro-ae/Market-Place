Once Reem Bank and Expleo have verified the build in UAT, Appro can deploy to production. We will release after the bureau go-live, not before — deploying ahead of it would put Score 3.0 codes in front of credit users while the bureau is still returning Score 2.1 values.
No interruption to Production when the ECB response is received. AECB has confirmed the response structure stays exactly as it is today — the score is still returned as an Index and a Range.
The impact is on how credit policy is set up. Score 3.0 is a new calibration with two scorecards on different scales — a score of 600 is High Risk on Mature but Low Risk on New to Credit. To help your team classify customers correctly, Appro has updated ECB Score Range in the Rule Engine and added a new ECB Score Segment, so each credit rule can be set against the right scale.
Updated data points and the upgraded report format will follow in the October release.
