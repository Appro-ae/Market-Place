Executive summary
One check, placed early, closes the gap
Where we are
A customer base the journey cannot see
Reem Bank holds a list of CIFs created manually in T24 by Operations — customers with an existing banking relationship who have not availed any product through the digital journey.
The gap
The journey assumes net-new customers
There is no controlled path for these customers today: the journey runs its full check chain and, at fulfilment, creates a new CIF in T24 — for a customer who already has one.
The proposal
Check Manual CIF — interim solution
A backend-configured list of manually-onboarded CIFs, checked at the start of the Pre-Dedupe step right after EID scanning. A match ends the journey gracefully — screen, Email/SMS, dedicated status, full audit visibility — and hands the customer to Operations.
3w
Tentative delivery: 2 weeks development + 1 week Reem Bank SIT, then deployment to RB UAT for Expleo testing — delivered within the dedicated Appro squad, no separate change request. BRD sign-off starts the clock.

Context
Two ways a customer exists in T24 today
1
Digitally onboarded
CIF created by the journey
The digital journey runs the full verification and decisioning chain and creates the CIF in T24 at fulfilment.
Fully visible to the Bank
Application, status, failed reasons and every step are available in Super Portal Enquiry and the Audit Trail.
2
Manually onboarded by Operations
CIF created directly in T24
An existing banking relationship, created by the Operations team — with no product availed through the digital journey.
Invisible to the journey today
The digital journey has no awareness of these CIFs — nothing recognises the relationship when such a customer starts an application.
→
Appro proposes an interim, configuration-led solution to handle this segment now. Maintaining the list on the Super Portal front end is a different customization with a longer timeline — available as a follow-on phase.

3
Proposed solution
Check Manual CIF — one check, three layers
1
Backend configuration
The Manual CIF list
The list of CIFs manually onboarded in T24 is configured in the backend — the so-called Check Manual CIF.
Part of the Pre-Dedupe step
The logic check is added into the Pre-Dedupe step, triggered after EID scanning — the earliest point at which the customer is identified.
2
Customer journey
Termination screen
The customer sees a termination screen — text and design prepared by Appro and shared for the Bank team's review.
Email / SMS
The customer is reassured in writing: the Bank already holds the relationship and the team will contact them to complete the account opening.
3
Back office & portal
Configurable communications
Email/SMS content is configured in Super Portal, so the text can be updated by the Bank.
New application status
A dedicated status for this group — to be defined in the BRD (e.g. within the Lead or Rejected family).
Enquiry & Audit Trail
Failed reasons and related data points are shown in Enquiry and the Audit Trail of the Super Portal.

Solution blueprint
Triggered at the earliest possible point
Customer scans EID — front & back
Journey steps 6–7. The scan identifies the customer — no integration call needed for the check itself.
▼
★ Check Manual CIF
New first gate of the Pre-Dedupe step: the scanned identity is checked against the configured Manual CIF list.
▼
Not on the list
The journey continues exactly as today — EFR verification, remaining Pre-Dedupe checks, Dedupe, AML, bureau and decisioning.
On the list
The journey ends gracefully — termination screen, Email/SMS, dedicated status with audit entry, Operations follow-up.
Why this point: on a match, EFR 1.2 / EFR 1.12, the remaining Pre-Dedupe checks and every other integration are no longer called — no spend on a journey that will not proceed.
