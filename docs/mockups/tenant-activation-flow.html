Member activation — end-to-end (Tenant URM BRD v2.2 §2.8.1–2.8.2)

Step 1 · Admin
Add user
Tenant Admin fills name, email, roles, environment and saves.
›
Step 2 · System
Status = Invited
Record created. No password is set and the member cannot sign in.
›
Step 3 · Email TT01
Invitation sent
Single-use …/activate?token= link, expires in 10 min (configurable).
›
Step 4 · Member
Activate Account
Sets own password, accepts Terms of Use. Token consumed.
›
Step 5 · System
Status = Active
Member signs in with email + password. Roles take effect.
What changed in v2.2. v2.1 created members straight to Active with no credential step and no email — steps 2–4 did not exist. The admin had to invent and hand over a password out-of-band. v2.2 makes the member set their own, and the account is only usable once they have.

The identity card is server-resolved. Email and organization come from the validated token, never from URL parameters — otherwise anyone could craft a link showing any organization name and have a ready-made phishing page on a trusted domain. Activate account stays disabled until password policy, confirm-match and the Terms tick all pass, so the member is never refused after committing a password.

This link has expired

Activation links can only be used once and are valid for a limited time. Request a fresh link and we'll email it to you.
Shown for expired, already-used and unrecognised tokens.
One message, three causes — deliberately. Expired, already-used and not-found all show the same screen so the page cannot be used to probe which email addresses exist. The trade-off: a member who has already activated is told their link expired rather than simply being sent to sign in — flagged as an open question in GAS-102 (OQ5) and worth a decision. AC4 covers the case the old spec missed entirely: a token that was valid at page load but expires while the member is still typing lands here on submit, with the resend one click away.
