02

One new variable. Two populations, told apart.

AECB Score Segment tells a strategy which scorecard produced the score — Mature or New to Credit — so a single rule can treat each one correctly.

1
2
1New variable on the criterion row2Two values: Mature / New to Credit
Why it matters
Available in Segmentation, Filtration and Deviation.
Also available as an attribute in Score Check Management.
GOOD TO KNOW

New to Credit has no Very Low Risk band — its scale tops out at 650. A rule that selects Very Low Risk, or tests a score above 650, will never match a New-to-Credit customer. Pair it with AECB Score Segment.

Score Range, right where your score checks live
