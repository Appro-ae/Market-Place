01 · Verdict

The document catalogue has converged — Portal v1.1 renamed its 23 documents to the exact 3806/3728 wording ("Liability letter from existing lender", "Pension evidence"…) and added the pensioner DOC024 row the profiles needed. But the queue model forked again: the portal's spec v1.2 keeps a 7-stage structure with the Settlement-to-Old-Lender, Clearance and Release controls — content-rich maker/checker checklists, per-stage TATs, an "Awaiting Old Bank" status, and documents that "release the hold" — while the v3 user stories ship flat queues with empty checklists and no release logic (F40). One of the two is the design intent; decide it before any of the four DM stories goes to dev.
