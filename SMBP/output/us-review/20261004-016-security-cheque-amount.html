Security Cheque — Amount Rule

How the security cheque amount should be calculated for B2B Personal Loan (AMP-3757, finding F31) — checked against the UAE regulation, market practice and the FABL deployment.

Recommendation: 120% × approved finance amount, income out4 Oct 2026 · Verified sources only
01 · The problem (F31)
RuleExample: salary 45,000 / loan 790,000Assessment
AMP-3757 today: lower of declared monthly income and 120% × finance amountmin(45,000 · 948,000) = AED 45,000 cheque on a 790,000 loanSecurity covers 5.7% of the exposure. Monthly income is an affordability measure, not a security basis — mixing the two breaks the cheque exactly when the loan is large.
Proposed: 120% × approved finance amount1.2 × 790,000 = AED 948,000Covers principal + interest, legal and admin cost on default. Sits exactly on the regulatory ceiling.
02 · Business base — regulation and market
SourceWhat it saysImplication
CBUAE Regulation 29/2011Banks and finance companies may only take from the customer post-dated cheques covering the installments, "of value not exceeding 120% of value of the loan or the debit balance".120% is a regulatory ceiling, not a formula — the system must validate ≤ 120%; the configured value decides the actual %.
ADJD Circular 1/2023 · Court of CassationA cheque-backed loan claim is admissible where the cheque covers up to 120% of the loan or debt; the security cheque stands as an independent guarantee.A cheque at 120% is enforceable in court as drafted — staying at or under the cap maximises enforceability.
Market practice (Gulf News) · DhanguardUAE banks take security cheques of 100–120% of the loan principal; the extra 20% covers interest, legal and administrative costs on default. Blank cheques are no longer accepted.Principal-based, never income-based. 120% is the common upper choice for unsecured PL.
03 · FABL precedent
TicketWhat FAB doesImplication for 3757
FABL-1000 (Done)The security cheque is a physical document collected from the customer and uploaded by the Disbursement Maker; the Checker validates it. Submit is blocked until it is uploaded. No amount is computed by the portal.FAB has no system formula — the bank fills the cheque per its credit policy. If SMBP generates the cheque digitally (3757's new approach), SMBP must define the formula FAB never needed.
FABL-1859 (Done)Cheque + first salary credit are mandatory before fund release for every customer who misses the Unicorn persona (Expat, private, ≥15k, ECB ≥711, <14×); app cannot leave the Maker queue without it.Confirms the cheque is a pre-release control tied to exposure, applied per persona — consistent with keeping it principal-based.
04 · Conclusion — proposed AC wording for AMP-3757

Drop monthly income from the formula. The regulation caps the cheque at 120% of the loan; the market writes it on the principal; FAB leaves the amount to the bank because the cheque is physical. Since SMBP generates the cheque, the rule must be explicit and principal-based.

Security Cheque Amount = Security Cheque % × Approved Finance Amount

1. Security Cheque % is a product-level configuration, default 120%, validated at 0.01%–120% (hard cap per CBUAE Regulation 29/2011).
2. Approved Finance Amount = the approved loan principal (for Buyout/Top Up: the total facility amount).
3. Monthly income plays no role in the cheque amount.
4. Rounding: up to the nearest AED 1 (never round a security down).

Example check: 790,000 loan → cheque AED 948,000 (not 45,000). A 100% bank preference is a config change, not a story change.
