AMP-3762 (+3761)

  1. Control chain: after Approve, Fund Holding runs and Loan Account Creation follows "straight away" — nothing waits for the cheque signature or the first salary credit, nothing captures the credit date, and no step releases the hold. The old stages 4–5 enforced exactly this. Please define the post-approval flow (hold-release trigger, credit-date capture, what blocks loan creation) or record the business decision to drop these controls.
  2. Routing: both IA1s defer routing to "a separate story" that has no key and isn't in the CR — the entry trigger, per-bank toggle, customer-wait transitions and TPQ paths from the old AC15 live nowhere. Please raise it and link it.
  3. Statuses: third naming generation — please register the final four (plus the customer-wait decision: status or TAT-excluded sub-state?) in the SSS model.
  4. Empty checklists: an empty checklist doesn't block Submit/Approve, and all four start empty — seed them at onboarding and require ≥1 active checker item before routing is switched On.
  5. Good: checker-≠-maker via Queue Assignment, entry-time checklist snapshot, ETL-based CIF check, and the channel "Verified" results only on final approval.

AMP-3757

  1. Dead references: ~8 citations of the deleted stage model (Stage 1 / Stage 4 / 3761 AC11-AC15). The cheque trigger is undefined in the four-queue model — suggest: generate when the Ops Maker's IBAN verifies AND persona = Not matched; regenerate per Ops re-submit; "Completed" per 3762 AC8.
