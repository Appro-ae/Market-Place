Super Portal vs Customer Journey — Gap Check

Cross-check of the SMBP Portal Mirror backend design — Document Management (MD13, profile builder) and the Ops/Fulfilment 5-stage disbursement queue (MD08S12/13) — against the CJ and Bank Portal user stories (AMP-3694 / 3404 / 3721 / 3728 / 3748 / 3689 / 3717 / 3744 / 3754). Both portal modules read at code level, in full.

Verdict: STRONG DESIGN, 6 P1 CONTRACT GAPS27 Sep 2026Nothing posted to Jira
01 · Verdict

The portal design is more advanced than the US in several places (ANY-of document groups, mutual-exclusivity engine, the 120% cheque cap, verified-not-typed disbursement details) — but it and the CJ stories currently describe three different document-resolution engines and two different persona rules. Six contract-level gaps must be reconciled before either side builds, or the CJ will send applications the portal cannot resolve.
