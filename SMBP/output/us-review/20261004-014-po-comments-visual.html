AMP-3757 — Security Cheque + SoC + Contract Pack

HOLD

Comment for the BA

  1. Cheque amount (AC3.2 #8): "lower of declared monthly income and 120% × finance" produces, in your own example, a 45,000 cheque securing a 790,000 loan (figure ①). A security cheque sizes on the loan, not one month's salary — expected: 120% × accepted Finance Amount (CBUAE Art. 7 cap). Please confirm the business base or remove the income term.

Either apply the EID signature image the customer already has in eKYC (the CJ screen ③ is built for exactly that) or declare the digital cheque evidential-only and name the real enforcement instrument. Needs legal's call.

HOLD

Comment for the BA

  1. 'U' kills DC for everyone: the G4 default blocked-status list includes U (Active Payments) — per AMP-3461's fixture that's the WorstStatus of every performing contract, so any customer with one active facility fails G4 and the DC card never appears (figure ①). Remove U; align with the DP's validated set.
