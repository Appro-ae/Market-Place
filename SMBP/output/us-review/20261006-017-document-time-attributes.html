Document Management — Time Attributes

Which document attributes need a time range in the Document Management module (AMP-3806 Document List, AMP-3728 profiles), checked against the portal's 53-document register and UAE market practice.

Gap: no time attribute exists anywhere today4 time attributes, 2 belong in DM config6 Oct 2026
01 · The gap

The Document List (3806), the profiles (3728) and the portal's 53-document register all carry the same attribute set — id, code, name, category, formats, stage, gate, active. No time attribute exists. The time rules are buried as free text in document names ("AECB credit report (within staleness limit)", "Payslips (last 3 months)") — unenforceable and invisible to the upload validation. Four time concepts need a home; only two of them belong in Document Management.

Recommendation to Kha: add two columns to the 3806 Document List config — Max Document Age (days, optional) and Expiry Check (Yes/No) — validated at customer upload and re-checked at Ops Maker. A3 values stay with the stories that generate those documents; A4 is one DMS retention policy, not a per-document column. Regulatory values (cooling-off 5 days, delay notice 10 days, retention 5 years) are fixed text, never editable config.
