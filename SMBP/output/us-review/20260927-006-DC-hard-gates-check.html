DC Hard Gates — Are the Rules Reflected Correctly?

The Bank Portal stories AMP-3732 (settlement, RtC) and AMP-3753 (config admin UI, To Do) checked gate-by-gate against the validated DP canon: 2215 base + ENH 2314/2316/2320 + defect fixes 3461/3457 (all UAT Validated) + open bug 3756. All read in full this session.

01 · Verdict

No — the hard rules are not reflected correctly. Three blockers: (1) the G4 default blocked-status list includes 'U' = Active Payments — the normal status of every performing contract, so every customer with any active facility fails G4 and DC is never offered; (2) G4's threshold re-introduces the band-code defect UAT already fixed in AMP-3461, with an impossible range (1–9 vs AECB codes 0–7) and a pass/fail boundary that contradicts between 3732 and 3753; (3) G5's validation re-introduces the exact regression fixed in AMP-3457 (ceiling up to 100% accepted; validated rule is 30–60) and is missing the rate/tenure inputs without which post-consolidation DBR cannot be computed at all.

P1'U' in the blocked-status default kills DC for everyone3753 AC6 G4 default list includes U (Active Payments) — AMP-3461's fixture shows performing contracts carry WorstStatus = 'U'. With 'U' blocked, any customer with one active contract fails G4 → DC variant never appears. Remove U (and review A/E/M/O/X — service statuses on a category that is always excluded from scope). P1G4 threshold re-introduces the AMP-3461 defect3461 (UAT Validated) ruled: band-code dropdown 0–7 preferred, never a raw number; display measured values. 3753 specs a numeric input with range 1–9 — 8 and 9 don't exist, code 0 can't be blocked, and the ambiguity 3461 closed is back. P1G4 boundary contradicts between the two BP stories3732: "no contract may have MaxDaysPaymentDelay ≥ 3" (code 3 fails). 3753: "maximum allowed delay bucket, default 3" (code 3 passes). Same gate, opposite outcome at the default. Pick one wording, state pass/fail at the boundary in both. Also: default 3 lets a 30–59-day delinquent (code 2) pass — the DP's post-3461 posture blocks from 30 days; justify the leniency or align.
P1G5 re-introduces the AMP-3457 D7 regression + cannot be computed3753 validates the ceiling as 0.01–100% — 3457 (UAT Validated) fixed exactly this: range must be 30–60 (50% = CBUAE retail cap). Since BP config is per PL product, the natural source is the product's own min rate/tenor (2320's Auto pattern) — but the story must say it, plus the income-null NC case and the "indicative, at standard tenure" label.
P2Default drift vs DP: G2 (2 vs 3) and G3 (30% vs 70%)3753 IA3 promises "field names, defaults and validation aligned to ensure consistency between portals" — the defaults on G2/G3 aren't. Align, or record the business reason per channel in both tickets.
