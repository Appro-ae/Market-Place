06

Decisions needed before Hailey builds

Where prototype and US disagree, the US wins by default — but each needs an explicit call so the prototype gets corrected, not silently ignored.
Conflict · Gấu trắng to confirm1 · Manual "Buyout details" screen vs AECB auto-fill

Prototype asks the customer to type outstanding balance / EMI / months left. US pulls everything from AECB. Recommendation: kill the manual screen — bureau data is the single source, matching the liability letter.

Conflict · Gấu trắng to confirm2 · Processing-fee card payment vs deduct-from-finance

Prototype has a 3-D Secure payment screen; AMP-3404 AC9 says B2B collects no payment — fees deduct at disbursal. Recommendation: no PSP in B2B scope; mark the screen B2C-only.

Watch · decide once3 · One signature model

Swipe-stamp (T&C/KFS), drawn signature (prototype), EFR signature + OTP (cheque). Pick the evidence model per document class before AMP-3757 is built.

Watch · config not copy4 · Numbers that must come from config

Min income (prototype "7,000"), re-apply block ("90 days"), grace range, liability-letter validity (default 15), fee lines in KFS. None may be hardcoded from prototype copy.

Watch · scope check5 · Manual document-upload eKYC route

Prototype offers upload-instead-of-scan; no AC covers it. Include only if the bank accepts assisted-KYC risk; otherwise drop.

Carry-over6 · Known spec gaps still open

G4 standing check for Buyout · settlement-execution (manager's cheque) workflow · cooling-off AC12 behaviours · BP-vs-DP gate defaults (G2/G3) · shortfall check trigger point (AMP-3732 AC6 vs AMP-3404 AC6a).
