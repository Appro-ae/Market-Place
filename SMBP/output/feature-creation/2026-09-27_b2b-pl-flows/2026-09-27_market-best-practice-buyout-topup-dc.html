UAE competitor benchmark

Six live products. Each card highlights the one practice worth copying or countering.

FAB — Buyout Loan

Buyout + funds
  • UAEN from ~5.95%, expat ~6.95% (variable, PBR-linked)
  • Min salary AED 7,000 · approved-employer list · salary transfer mandatory
  • Max AED 5M UAEN / 2M expat · tenor ≤48m (≤60m MoD)
Grace up to 365 days (UAEN) / 275 days (expat) before the first instalment — grace as the headline acquisition weapon.
bankfab.com · Paisabazaar

Emirates NBD — Buy Out

Buyout
  • Management fee 1% of finance amount (capped)
  • Requires existing loan in good standing + balance / rate / term details
Digital self-serve liability letters — the settlement document is a product feature, not a branch errand.
emiratesnbd.com · liability letters

ADCB — buyout programme

Buyout + top-up
  • Settles other banks directly and refinances at ADCB rate
  • Old-bank exit fee 1%, capped AED 10,000
Top-up bundled by default — the extra cash is the sales hook, the buyout is the vehicle.
Soulwallet

Mashreq — Debt Consolidation

DC
  • Min salary 5,000 (listed cos.) / 8,000 (non-listed) — two-tier ALOC pattern
  • Salary transfer required · merges loans + cards + overdraft
Markets exactly our pitch: "one payment, lower rate".
mashreq.com

DIB — Liability Settlement Finance

DC · Islamic
  • Named Islamic DC product settling existing liabilities
  • Structured via Tawarruq / Murabaha — profit, not interest
Validates our Islamic wording rule — and raises the Tawarruq settlement-flow question (§04).
dib.ae

Finance House — Buyout

Buyout
  • Non-bank lender · buyout without salary transfer for some segments
Salary transfer is a pricing lever, not a universal precondition — matches our STL-as-factor design.
financehouse.ae

04

Conclusions for backend design

Rationale → evidence → action. Three gaps, one opportunity, one risk.
ParityStructure is right — no redesign

Direct settlement, bureau-driven selection, savings framing all match market leaders. Build as specified.

Gap 1Buyout needs a standing check

Market gates buyout on "in good standing"; our G4 covers DC only. Extend G4 (or a filtration rule) to Buyout variants.

Gap 2No settlement-execution story

Manager's cheque issuance, settlement status, reconciliation — the #1 complaint area in UAE buyouts — has no US. Raise as new scope.

Gap 3Letter-validity default = 15 days

Config exists (1–365) but ships without a default. UAE norm is 15 days — set it.

OpportunityGrace period as a weapon

FAB acquires with up to 365-day grace; our fallback caps at 30–99. Make the ceiling configurable so client banks can match.

RiskIslamic tenant = Tawarruq flow

For an Islamic bank, settlement legally flows from the customer's commodity-sale proceeds, not a bank payment. Confirm contract generation and settlement instructions before build.
