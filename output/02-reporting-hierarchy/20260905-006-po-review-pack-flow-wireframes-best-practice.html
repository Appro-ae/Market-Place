Feature flow · wireframes on the live portal · design decisions vs market best practice

What v4 delivers in one sentence: every user gets an immutable Staff ID and a Reporting Manager; every new application is tagged to a Staff ID at origination (UTM code or sales round-robin); enquiry screens show staff their own cases, managers their whole subtree, and View-All roles everything — governed by maker/checker, lifecycle guards and audit.

Method. Each v4 design decision is compared with how the reference products in the market implement the same concept — Salesforce (role hierarchy, queues, assignment rules, ownership transfer) and Microsoft Dynamics 365 (hierarchy security, assignment rules) — plus banking dual-control norms. Verdicts: Aligned follows market practice · Consider works, but market handles it more robustly · Gap market-standard capability missing.

1Ownership key = personImmutable Staff ID per user; application tagged to one Staff IDSalesforce and Dynamics both model access around a single record Owner (user); hierarchy access is derived from the owner.AlignedCorrect foundation. Person-level tag is what makes both visibility and sales reporting work.
2Manager visibility via hierarchyFull reporting subtree, unlimited depth, real-timeSalesforce role hierarchy: users see all data owned by users below them (no depth cap). Dynamics manager hierarchy: configurable Depth limits how many levels down a manager sees; Microsoft recommends keeping the effective hierarchy ≤50 users under a manager for performance.ConsiderUnlimited depth matches Salesforce, but Dynamics' depth setting exists for a reason — performance and over-exposure at the top of large orgs. Ask dev for a materialized subtree (closure table) and keep a configurable depth cap in the back pocket.
3Read vs act on subordinates' casesAC9: "existing actions remain unchanged" — anyone who sees a row can use its actions (e.g. Cancel Application on the detail page)Dynamics distinguishes: direct reports → Read/Write; non-direct reports deeper in the chain → read-only.ConsiderDecide explicitly whether a Head of Sales may cancel a case three levels down. Market default is read-only beyond direct reports; at minimum state the rule in AC9.
4Bypass for operations"View All Applications" role permission, default off, Enquiry-onlySalesforce "View All" object permission and Dynamics organization-level access serve exactly this purpose; both are role/profile-level.AlignedCorrect scoping (Enquiry only, no User Management rights).
5Unassigned casesUntagged → visible to View-All only + alert email to opsSalesforce Queues: unassigned records are owned by a queue; queue members and users above them in the hierarchy can view and take ownership. Assignment rules route to a user or a queue.ConsiderMarket puts unassigned leads where sales can pick them up; v4 parks them with ops. Consider an "Unassigned" sales pool visible to sales managers (queue pattern) — an alert email alone does not convert a lead.
6Auto-assignment methodLoad-based: lowest attributed count, A→Z tiebreak, pool per channel/productDynamics 365 Sales assignment rules offer both round robin (turn-based on last assignment) and load balancing (capacity-based), plus availability and capacity flags. Salesforce lead assignment rules route by criteria to users/queues.AlignedLoad-based is a recognised market option. Phase 2 candidates from the market: seller availability (leave) and capacity caps.
7Ownership transferAttribution immutable; no reassignment function (AC8 IEM004 nevertheless asks for cases to be "reassigned")Salesforce: Change Owner per record and Mass Transfer Records; deactivation guidance is to transfer records owned by the leaving user. Dynamics: Assign action on any owned record.GapThe one market-standard capability missing. Ownership transfer with audit is universal in CRM/LOS; needed for leavers, corrections and territory moves. (Known open point — small AC to add.)
8Leaver handlingDeactivation blocked until reports re-pointed (IEM003) and open cases reassigned (IEM004)Salesforce lets records stay owned by an inactive user and recommends transferring them; v4 is stricter by design.AlignedStricter than market — good for a bank — but only workable once item 7 exists.
9Hierarchy changes are real-timeRM change recalculates visibility immediately; historical cases move with the userSalesforce role changes take effect on the user's next access (real-time from a business standpoint); sharing is recalculated by the platform.AlignedNote for reporting: manager roll-ups shift when the org chart changes. The immutable origination tag already preserves individual sales credit — good.

Recommendation: Approve for development with three explicit decisions recorded, and a named phase-2 backlog. The design follows market practice on ownership, hierarchy access, ops bypass, assignment, audit and dual control — stronger than CRM norms on governance. The differences are choices, not mistakes, and should be recorded as such.

Decision 1 — Manager rights beyond read

Can a manager act (e.g. Cancel Application) on cases of indirect reports? Market default: read-only beyond direct reports. State the rule in AC9.

Decision 2 — Where do unassigned cases live?

Ops alert only (v4) or an "Unassigned" pool visible to sales managers (queue pattern) so leads get worked, not just reported.

Decision 3 — Ownership transfer

Add the small "Reassign Case (Sale Staff)" AC (permission-gated, maker/checker, audited). It is the only market-standard capability missing and IEM004 already assumes it exists.

Phase-2 backlog (from the market comparison)

Attribution

  • QR / manual staff-code capture for assisted journeys (ADIB pattern)
  • Seller availability and capacity awareness in round-robin (Dynamics pattern)
  • Controlled Staff ID correction that re-points tags (instead of deactivate + recreate)

Visibility & scale

  • Delegated access / leave cover and branch-level sharing
  • Configurable hierarchy depth cap + materialized subtree for performance
  • Restriction criteria and date for removing View-All from staff roles after go-live
